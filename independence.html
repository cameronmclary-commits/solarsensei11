Affiliate and Independence Disclosure

Last updated: September 2026

Solar Sensei maintains a separation between its analysis tool and the installers it connects homeowners with. We do not receive commissions from installer referrals. Our recommendations are based on your data, not installer relationships.

How Solar Sensei works

Solar Sensei is an independent analysis tool. It models solar and battery configurations based on your electricity usage, tariff, and household details. It does not sell panels, batteries, or installation services.

Installer selection

Where Solar Sensei connects homeowners with installers, this is based on installer reputation, track record, and business stability — not on commission arrangements or commercial partnerships. Solar Sensei works with a small number of vetted installers in each area.

No financial interest

Solar Sensei does not receive commissions, referral fees, or other financial incentives from installers. The service is free for homeowners. Installer referral is a courtesy to help homeowners find reputable providers after completing their analysis.

Independent inspection

Where independent inspection is offered through Solar Sensei, this is provided by a separate qualified inspector — not by the installer who performed the work. This ensures the inspection is genuinely independent.

Content independence

Guides, articles, and educational content on this website are written independently and are not sponsored by any installer, manufacturer, or industry body. Opinions are based on real-world consultation experience across thousands of homeowner appointments.
