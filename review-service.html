INDEPENDENT REVIEW SERVICE

Your solar system, independently assessed.

A thorough, expert-led inspection designed to uncover hidden faults, performance issues, and money-saving opportunities in your current setup.

THE PROBLEM

60% of solar installations are substandard

Installers marking their own homework. No independent check. Homeowners with no way to verify performance vs what was promised.

No independent verification

The installer designs, installs, and self-certifies. There's no second set of eyes checking whether the system matches what was sold.

Faults take years to surface

By the time you notice lower-than-expected output, the installer may be gone. Damage from water ingress, burnt isolators, or poor wiring has already accumulated.

Your bill doesn't tell the whole story

Lower bills after install can mask underperformance. A 6.6kW system producing 4kW looks fine on your bill — until you realise you overpaid and undersaved.

THE TECHNOLOGY GAP

Existing tools don't model your options accurately

After thousands of in-person solar consultations, one pattern was clear: the software installers use isn't built to help you make a good decision.

Plan changes bundled into projections

Solar design software often bundles an electricity plan change into the savings projection. This inflates the result and hides the true value of the system alone — making it impossible to compare options fairly.

Battery-plan compatibility overlooked

Not every battery works with every plan. Some tariffs restrict battery charging or export. Design software rarely models which batteries can actually connect to which plans — leading to mismatched recommendations.

Single-design bias

Software is built to sell one design, not compare many. It doesn't test 241+ combinations against your real usage. It shows you one option and tells you it's the best.

Generic assumptions

Average consumption. Average sunlight. Average family. Your home isn't average — and a tool built on averages will give you an average result.

TWO CHECKS, NOT ONE

Analysis tells you what to buy. Inspection tells you what you got.

Both are needed. One without the other leaves a gap.

WHAT'S INSPECTED

A thorough, expert-led assessment

Our fully-qualified solar technicians assess your entire system.

1. Solar Panel Testing

Visual inspection for damage, dirt or wear. Electrical testing to ensure each panel is functioning properly and safely. Performance compared to rated output.

2. Inverter Testing

The heart of your solar system. We assess whether it's working correctly, converting power efficiently, and check for error codes or performance drops against rated capacity.

3. Switchboard & Wiring

Electrical connections inspected for safety, compliance, and risk of faults caused by poor workmanship or wear and tear. DC isolator condition checked — burnt isolators are a fire risk.

4. Power Bill Review

Your latest electricity bill and solar output compared. We check whether the system is performing as it should and identify whether you're getting the return on investment you expected.

COMMON FAULTS

Issues that show up time and time again

Catching them early prevents costly failures — and safety hazards.

Electrical faults

  • Fire risk Burnt DC isolators
  • Fire risk Melted MC4 solar plugs
  • Fire risk Melted solar wiring
  • Corrosion Water ingress into DC isolators
  • Safety Incorrect cable used for solar wiring

Installation errors

  • Compliance DC isolators installed incorrectly
  • Performance Inverter in wrong location
  • Documentation Incorrect solar labels
  • Weathering Panel connection covers missing
  • Safety No earthing to panels or array frame

Panel & safety issues

  • Damage Shattered glass on solar modules
  • Degradation Burn lines on solar cells under glass
  • Efficiency Excessive dirt or debris buildup
  • Structural Mounting frame corrosion or looseness
  • Documentation Missing installation records
WHEN TO GET INSPECTED

Six reasons to book an inspection

New installation

Before making final payment. Verify the system matches the design and performs as promised.

Buying property with solar

Don't assume the existing system is good. An inspection reveals what you're actually inheriting.

Bills don't match expectations

If savings are lower than projected, an inspection identifies whether it's the system, the plan, or both.

System is 5+ years old

Components degrade. Wiring ages. Early inspection catches problems before they become expensive.

Before adding a battery

Your existing system needs to be sound before adding storage. An inspection ensures the foundation is solid.

Suspected performance issues

Shading, panel damage, inverter faults, or wiring issues — an inspection finds the cause.
