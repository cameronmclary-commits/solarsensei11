FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before requesting your Solar Sensei Report.

Is this just a solar quote?

No. Solar Sensei is a personalised report covering usage, current plan, alternative plans, solar, battery, savings attribution, and long-term outlook.

What is the difference between $0 and $90?

Both include the same full Solar Sensei Report. The $90 option adds a 45-minute consultation for interpretation, quote comparison and next-step guidance.

What data do I need to upload?

Time-of-Use data is most common and sufficient for modelling. If you can provide higher-resolution interval data (30-minute readings) we will use it for even finer analysis. Export as CSV from your retailer portal — 12 months recommended where available.

Why do you need interval data?

Because timing matters. Usage timing affects whether plan switching, solar, battery storage or a combined solution is best.

Will my current plan be included?

Yes. Your current plan is the baseline so you can see what you pay today and how other options compare.

Can I use this if I already have solar?

Yes. Solar Sensei can analyse export behaviour, self-consumption, plan fit, and whether a battery retrofit may be worthwhile.

Does this require a solar quote first?

No. Solar Sensei works with or without existing quotes. If you have quotes, we'll include them in the comparison to see how they stack up against other configurations.

Is the report tied to specific products or installers?

No. Solar Sensei is vendor-neutral. We model hardware-agnostic configurations and do not receive referral fees from installers or manufacturers.

How long does the analysis take?

Reports are typically generated within 24 hours of data upload. Complex scenarios with multiple tariff comparisons or battery brand modelling may take up to 48 hours.

Are savings guaranteed?

No. Savings are projections based on available data, rates, assumptions, weather, usage behaviour, site conditions and future energy prices.
