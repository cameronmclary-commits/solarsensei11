What's included

  • 4 Power Bill Review — performance vs expectations, ROI check

Common faults we find

These issues show up time and time again during inspections. Catching them early prevents costly failures.

Electrical faults

Burnt DC isolators, water ingress, melted MC4 plugs, melted wiring, and incorrect cable used for solar wiring.

Installation errors

DC isolators installed incorrectly, inverter in the wrong location, incorrect solar labels, and missing panel connection covers.

Panel & safety issues

Shattered glass on modules, burn lines on solar cells, and no earthing to panels or array frame.

What happens after the inspection?

You receive clear, expert feedback on what's working, what needs attention, and how to make the most of your solar setup.

System expansion
