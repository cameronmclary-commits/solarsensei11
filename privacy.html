Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information we collect

Solar Sensei collects information you provide directly, including electricity bill data, household details, and contact information when you use our analysis tool or contact us.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to provide solar and battery analysis, generate your personalised report, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data storage

Your bill data and analysis results are processed to generate your report. We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information.

4. Third-party services

We may use third-party services for website hosting, analytics, and payment processing. These services have access to information only as needed to perform their functions.

5. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to maintain functionality and improve your experience. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. Contact us at cameronmclary@gmail.com to make a request.
