WHAT'S INCLUDED

Understanding when you use power can be as important as how much is used.

Two homes can use the same annual electricity and need completely different solutions. Solar Sensei identifies whether the opportunity is plan switching, solar self-consumption, battery peak offset, feed-in credits, or a combination.

Energy fingerprint

Seasonal patterns, peak-cost windows and average-day usage profiles.

Plan analysis

Current plan reconstruction, plan fit score, tariff exposure and alternative plan comparison.

Solar modelling

Solar match, size comparison, generation, self-consumption, export and diminishing returns.

Battery modelling

Battery value window, size comparison, utilisation, backup potential and day-in-the-life simulation.

Financial forecast

Savings waterfall, attribution donut, payback curve, 10-year benefit and sensitivity analysis.

Recommendation matrix

Best overall, fastest payback, lowest bill, best backup and plan-switch-only pathways.
