Why this matters

Going solar should not begin with a sales assumption.

Installer quotes often show what a selected system might cost. They do not always establish whether that system, a different configuration, or no purchase at all produces the best result for your household.

Your usage is not an average

Timing matters. Morning peaks, overnight baseload, weekends and seasonal variation can materially change which system performs best.

One quote is not a comparison

A single proposed configuration cannot show how it ranks against smaller, larger, battery and solar-only alternatives.

The tariff can change the answer

Usage rates, feed-in rates and time-of-use periods may influence value as much as the hardware itself.

SIGNATURE INSIGHT

Your usage timing matters more than total usage.

Two homes can use the same annual electricity and need completely different solutions. Solar Sensei identifies whether the opportunity is plan switching, solar self-consumption, battery peak offset, feed-in credits, or a combination.

Example Solar Sensei finding

Evening imports represent 31% of imported energy but 46% of usage charges. That is why the recommended 16 kWh battery targets the 5pm–9pm peak window instead of simply maximising solar size.

FAQ

Common questions

▾ Is this just a solar quote?

No. Solar Sensei is a personalised energy analysis across usage, current plan, alternative plans, solar, battery, savings attribution, and long-term outlook.

▾ What is the difference between $0 and $90?

Both include the same full report. The $90 option adds a 45-minute consultation for interpretation, quote comparison, and next-step guidance.

▾ Why do you need interval data?

Because timing matters. Time-of-Use data is sufficient for most homes. If you provide higher-resolution interval data (30-min), we use it for finer peak-window and export analysis. Either way, timing affects whether plan switching, solar, battery, or a combination is best.

▾ Will my current plan be included?

Yes. Your current plan is the baseline so you can see what you pay today and how other options compare.

▾ Can I use this if I already have solar?

Yes. Solar Sensei can analyse export behaviour, self-consumption, plan fit, and whether a battery retrofit may be worthwhile.

▾ Are savings guaranteed?

No. Savings are projections based on available data, rates, assumptions, weather, usage behaviour, site conditions, and future energy prices.
