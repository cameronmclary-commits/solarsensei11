FAQ

Common questions

Is this just a solar quote?

No. Solar Sensei is a personalised report covering usage, current plan, alternative plans, solar, battery, savings attribution, and long-term outlook.

What is the difference between $0 and $90?

Both include the full report. The $90 option adds a 45-minute consultation and quote comparison support.

Why do you need interval data?

Because timing matters. Usage timing affects whether plan switching, solar, battery storage or a combined solution is best.

Will my current plan be included?

Yes. Your current plan is the baseline so you can see what you pay today and how other options compare.

Can I use this if I already have solar?

Yes. Solar Sensei can analyse export behaviour, self-consumption, plan fit, and whether a battery retrofit may be worthwhile.

Are savings guaranteed?

No. Savings are projections based on data, rates and assumptions. Actual outcomes can vary.
